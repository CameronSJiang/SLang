# Word frequency lists

`*.json.gz` in this folder are the most common words per language (most frequent first),
generated by `scripts/gen-freq.py` from **wordfreq** 3.1.1 by Robyn Speer
(https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq), which combines data from OpenSubtitles, Wikipedia,
Google Books Ngrams, Reddit, Twitter, SUBTLEX and other corpora.

The wordfreq data is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
license (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). These derived lists (lowercased,
filtered to word-like entries, truncated to 20,000 words) are distributed under the same
license.
