Questions

The short answers.

Which languages does SLang support?

Spanish is the star right now, including the natural dub voice. Captions and word meanings also work for French, Japanese, Korean and English, with more on the way.

Is my data private?

Yes. The words you know, the videos you watch and your saved words stay on your computer. SLang has no account, no tracking and no server of its own.

Does it work on Netflix or my phone?

Not yet. SLang works on YouTube in Chrome on your computer. Tell us on the waitlist form where you’d want it next.

What do I get for joining the waitlist?

Early access to SLang Pro before anyone else, and a say in what we build next. No spam, just the occasional update.
