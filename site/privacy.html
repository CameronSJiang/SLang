Privacy policy · last updated November 26, 2026

What SLang keeps, and what it sends

Short version: SLang stores everything it learns about you in your own browser. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no server. Data leaves your computer only in the cases listed under “What leaves your computer”.

What SLang stores on your computer

  • Subtitles of the YouTube videos you watch with SLang on, and the translations and word meanings made from them, so a video doesn’t have to be translated twice.
  • Your word list: words you looked up, saved, marked as known or ignored, the sentence and video moment they came from, and SLang’s estimate of which words you know.
  • Quiz and review history for saved words.
  • Settings, including your languages and level, and an Anthropic API key if you choose to add one.

This lives in the extension’s browser storage (IndexedDB and chrome.storage.local). It is not synced and not sent to us. Uninstalling SLang deletes it.

What leaves your computer

  • Only if you add your own Anthropic API key: the text of the subtitles being translated, and the names of your two languages, are sent to Anthropic’s API (api.anthropic.com) to be translated. Anthropic handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy. Remove the key in SLang’s settings to stop this.
  • If you download the natural voice: SLang downloads the voice model files (about 325 MB, or 90 MB on computers without a usable graphics chip) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). This is an ordinary file download; nothing about you or what you watch is included.
  • Chrome’s built-in AI, which SLang uses for translation by default, runs on your computer. Chrome may download its model, which is governed by Google’s policies.

What SLang does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry or tracking.
  • No ads, and no selling or sharing of data.
  • No account and no SLang server.
  • It doesn’t read pages other than YouTube unless you click “Show captions on this page”.

Why SLang asks for its permissions

  • youtube.com: to read video subtitles and show SLang’s captions on the player.
  • storage: to keep your settings on your computer.
  • activeTab and scripting: to show captions on another site’s video, only on the tab you choose, only when you click the button.
  • offscreen: to run translation and the voice model in the background.

This website and the waitlist

This site is hosted by Netlify, which keeps standard server logs. The waitlist and survey use Google Forms; if you leave your email there, it’s used only to tell you about SLang, and you can ask for it to be removed at any time.

Planned paid features

Planned Pro features such as cloud translation will need to send subtitles to a server. Before they launch, this policy will be updated to say exactly what is sent, to whom, and how long it is kept.

Children

SLang isn’t directed at children under 13.
